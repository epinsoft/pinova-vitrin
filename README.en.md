## ⭐ Highlights
- ⚡ **Fully automatic delivery** — stock code delivered within seconds after payment
- 🔐 **AES-encrypted stock** — codes stored encrypted in the database
- 💳 **Wallet + payments** — balance top-up, multiple payment gateways (connect your own)
- 🧑‍💼 **Reseller system** — dealer pricing and panel
- 🎁 **Coupons · campaigns · draws** — conversion & loyalty tools
- 🤖 **AI blog** — content generation with OpenAI / Gemini / Claude
- 📊 **Admin panel** — products/stock/orders/reports, dozens of pages
- 📱 **Mobile-friendly**, SEO-ready (schema, sitemap, blog)

## 🧩 Tech
PHP (CodeIgniter 3) · MySQL/MariaDB · server-side rendering. Payment gateways are modular; you connect your own provider.
